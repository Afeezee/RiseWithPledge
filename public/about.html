About the Foundation

Committed to creating opportunities

RISE WITH PLEDGE EDUCATION WELFARE AND EMPOWERMENT FOUNDATION is a nonprofit and community-focused organisation committed to creating opportunities for vulnerable children, youths, women and communities through education, welfare support, capacity building, mentorship, and social intervention programmes.

Through strategic partnerships, volunteerism, advocacy, and community engagement, we seek to inspire hope, create opportunities, and promote lasting positive change.

Our Impact Goals

  • Improve access to education.
  • Support vulnerable individuals and families.
  • Promote economic empowerment.
  • Strengthen communities through sustainable initiatives.
  • Raise future leaders and changemakers.

“At RISE WITH PLEDGE, we believe that true development begins when people are empowered with knowledge, opportunities, and support. Our mission is to inspire change, uplift lives, and create pathways for individuals and communities to thrive. Together, we can build a future where no one is left behind.”

Barrister Abimbola Adebayo, Esq. Founder / Executive Director

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

Measuring our impact, improving our work

At RISE WITH PLEDGE, we are committed to measuring our impact and continuously improving our programmes. We collect and analyze data through beneficiary feedback, project reports, surveys, stakeholder consultations, and periodic evaluations to ensure our interventions create meaningful and sustainable change.

Our MEL framework helps us:

  • Track progress towards programme objectives.
  • Measure outcomes and impact.
  • Ensure accountability to beneficiaries, donors, and partners.
  • Identify lessons learned and best practices.
  • Improve future programme design and implementation.
Safeguarding Policy

Protecting the dignity, safety, and rights of everyone we serve

RISE WITH PLEDGE EDUCATION WELFARE AND EMPOWERMENT FOUNDATION is committed to protecting the welfare, dignity, safety, and rights of all beneficiaries, volunteers, staff members, and stakeholders.

🚫 Zero-tolerance approach to:

  • Abuse
  • Exploitation
  • Harassment
  • Discrimination
  • Bullying
  • Neglect

🛡 Our safeguarding commitment:

  • Respecting the dignity and rights of all persons.
  • Protecting children and vulnerable adults from harm.
  • Promoting safe and inclusive environments.
  • Responding promptly and appropriately to safeguarding concerns.
  • Ensuring ethical conduct by staff, volunteers, and partners.

All staff, volunteers, consultants, and partners are expected to uphold these safeguarding principles.
